How to cancel Plus

You can cancel anytime in your iPhone Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Pennie, then Cancel Subscription. Your Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for, and your entries stay on your phone.

Your data

Your data stays on this iPhone. We don't have servers that store it. Your entries move with your iPhone backup in iCloud or on a computer. You can also export a backup file in Export and import, then import it on the new iPhone.

Not medical advice

Pennie is a journal for your own records. It doesn't give medical advice and never suggests a dose. Your doctor or pharmacist decides your medication and dose. If something feels wrong, contact your doctor. In an emergency, call 911.
