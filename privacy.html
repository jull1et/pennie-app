Pennie

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

The short version

Pennie is made by Yuliya Ustiantseva, an independent developer. Pennie is a journal for your GLP-1 shots. Everything you log stays on your iPhone. We don't have servers that store your entries, we can't see them, and we don't sell or share them.

There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your medication, dose and schedule, shots and injection sites, check-ins, side effects, weight, protein, fiber and water, notes and settings. They are saved in a database inside the app on your iPhone.

Your iPhone backup (iCloud or a computer) can include this database, under Apple's backup settings. Files you export with Export CSV or Export backup go wherever you send or save them.

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They say "your shot" and include your medication name only if you turn on Show medication name.

Purchases

Apple handles payment for Pennie Plus. We never see your card or Apple ID details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether Plus is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app ID, your purchase details and basic device information such as the iOS version and App Store country. It never receives your health entries. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Consumer health data

Under US state laws on consumer health data, such as Washington's My Health My Data Act: we don't collect, share or sell your consumer health data. Your health entries stay on your device and under your control.

Your choices

You can see and edit all your entries in Journal, export them in Settings → Export and import, and delete everything in Settings → Privacy → Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone.

Pennie is meant for adults 18 and older.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date below and show it in the app. Questions or requests: julliet.cooperation@gmail.com.
